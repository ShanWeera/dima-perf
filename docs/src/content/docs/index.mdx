## What DiMA does

For every overlapping k-mer position of a viral protein or gene alignment, DiMA counts the
k-mers and the sequences that support them, estimates their Shannon entropy with the paper's
correction for sample size, classifies variants as Index, Major, Minor and Unique motifs with
their incidences, can count them per host, country, year or any header field, and finds the
historically conserved sequences of the alignment [@tharanga2025]. On request it also computes
similarity-weighted entropy, an addition to the paper's method in which near-identical variants
count for less [@wu2025].

## Cite DiMA

If you use DiMA, please cite the paper and the version you used; `dima cite` prints both, and
`dima cite --from results.json` the references of a run. See [Citing DiMA](/reference/citing/).
